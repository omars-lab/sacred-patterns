The polygon-anchored escape hatch (Ticks 69 PASS at N=1 lens, 72-74 PASS at N=1/2/3 lenses on shared C0) only holds when polygon vertices and all-arc convergence vertices are SPATIALLY DISJOINT. If even ONE polygon vertex P doubles as an all-arc convergence with hazardous topology (degree-6 + co-tangent pair), the polygon edges incident at P do NOT suppress the OVER-emission contamination — instead the polygon face itself gets absorbed into the polyclass cross-mix, leaving zero pure polygon-class faces.

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 81 (2026-05-27) authored `square-and-dual-lens-cotangent-at-vertex.bkr.tmpl` as the boundary probe between the safe pattern (Ticks 69/72-74) and the hazardous all-arc cell (Tick 80). Geometry: C0=(0,0) r=100 ÷4 → square inscribed at vertices (±100,0)/(0,±100); CL_shared=(50,50) r=70.7107 tangent 45° at P=(100,0); CL_a=(40.9115,-10.4189) r=60 tangent 100° at P; CL_b=(139.3923,6.9459) r=40 tangent 100° at P (co-tangent with CL_a — Tick 77 hazard). Vertex P=(100,0) has degree-6 incidence: 2 square edges (cpt3→cpt0 at 45°, cpt0→cpt1 at 135°) + 2 CL_shared arcs + 2 sets of {CL_a, CL_b} arcs. **Predicted three risks: (i) BOUNDARY-SUPPRESSION (7 shapes clean), (ii) BOUNDARY-IRRELEVANT (≥7 shapes with .square leakage), (iii) NOVEL MIXED-LINE-ARC FINGERPRINT (square absorbed / 3-sided / mixed line+arc). ACTUAL outcome: hybrid (ii)+(iii)** — 8 shapes (4 polygonal type=unknown + 4 circles), every polygonal face has face_class=None, every polygonal face source_primitives contains BOTH `.square` AND at least one `.lens_*` tag. **Concrete contamination:** shape[0]=`[.lens_b×3, .square×2, C0:arc:#2, C0:arc:#3×2, square_poly×2]`; shape[2]=`[.lens_a×5, .lens_b, .square×2, C0:arc:#0×2, C0:arc:#1×3, C0:arc:#3, square_poly×2]` (every lens identity + square present); shape[3]=`[.lens_a×2, .square×4, C0:arc:#0, C0:arc:#1, square_poly×4]`. No pure `.square` face survives — the square's interior gets partitioned into 4 mixed sub-faces by the lens arcs passing through its corner.

**The mechanism (inferred):** when polygon vertex P doubles as all-arc convergence, the bikar face-walker's tangent-sort defect at degree-6 (Tick 75) and co-tangent tie-break (Tick 79) propagate THROUGH the polygon-edge cycle. The polygon-edge enrollment (Tenet 9 polyclass trap precursor: shared `.square` tag on cpt3→cpt0 + cpt0→cpt1) gets absorbed into the lens-arc cycles emerging from P, because the face-walker walks edges by tangent-sorted ordering at each vertex regardless of edge-type (line vs arc). At P, the tangent sort puts the 6 incident half-edges in an order that mixes polygon edges with arc edges, and each resulting closed cycle inherits source_primitives from EVERY edge it traverses. Result: 4 faces, each tagged with the union of `.square` + (subset of `.lens_a`, `.lens_b`).

**How to apply:** (a) **The polygon-anchored escape hatch ONLY holds when polygon vertices are SPATIALLY DISJOINT from any hazardous all-arc convergence.** If any polygon vertex P is also an all-arc convergence with degree-6+ AND co-tangent arc-pair, the polygon face WILL be absorbed and the safety claim is voided; (b) when authoring N≥2 lenses converging at a vertex P, check whether P is ALSO a polygon vertex BEFORE relying on the polygon-anchoring safety claim — if P coincides with a polygon corner, the escape hatch doesn't apply; (c) Tick 81's fingerprint (square absorbed, all faces polyclass-mixed with .square + .lens_*) is the diagnostic signature for "you thought you had polygon-anchored safety but the vertex is shared with the hazardous all-arc topology"; (d) downstream consumers attempting class recovery from source_primitives CANNOT recover the `.square` identity from Tick 81's fingerprint because EVERY face mixes `.square` with `.lens_*` — the consumer must detect "polygon class tag present in all polygonal faces" upstream and route to a separate analysis path; (e) **the SAFE design rule for compositions:** place polygon vertices on circle-division points that are NOT lens-convergence points; if a lens must converge at a polygon corner, the polygon's class tag will leak into every emergent face; (f) **the witness IS the limitation** — do NOT file as a bikar bug to chase; the current behavior is graceful degradation (correct circle emission, marked-unknown polyclass faces) rather than crash.
