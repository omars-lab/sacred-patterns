When two non-shared circles in a multi-lens topology cross at TWO points where only ONE is declared via `intersect` (e.g., CL_a + CL_b both pass through shared vertex P, and also cross at a 2nd unrelated point Q), bikar's face-walker does NOT detect or split arcs at the undeclared crossing Q. The "stray" Q stays dormant in the DCEL — no vertex created, no edge split, no sub-face emission. The face-walker respects the `intersect`-declaration boundary even when sweep-line or arc-traversal could plausibly discover Q via geometric incidence.

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 78 (2026-05-27) authored `dual-lens-shared-vertex-homogeneous-non-cotangent.bkr.tmpl`. Three circles r=50: CL_shared, CL_a at angle 60° from P, CL_b at angle -100° from P (all through P=(100,0)). The pattern only declares `intersect XLA CL_shared CL_a` + `intersect XLB CL_shared CL_b` — not `intersect XAB CL_a CL_b`. **Geometrically, CL_a + CL_b cross at TWO points: P=(100,0) AND a stray at (116.3176, -5.9391).** Predicted: stray stays dormant per local-arc-enrollment rule (Ticks 69-74). **Confirmed:** 5 shapes emitted (1 lens_a + 1 lens_b + 3 circles), clean source_primitives partition IDENTICAL to Tick 23. No sub-face emerged from stray-induced arc subdivision. The face-walker did not detect or react to the stray.

**Rule boundary refined (combined with Ticks 69-74):**
- **Ticks 69-74 (original local-arc-enrollment rule):** within a single shared circle, sub-arcs at multiple potential cut points stay dormant unless enrolled. Established for 1 lens (Ticks 69-71) and N lenses (Ticks 72-74) on a shared C0.
- **Tick 78 (this generalization):** the rule extends to crossings BETWEEN two non-shared circles. If two circles cross at points P1 (enrolled) and P2 (unenrolled), the face-walker treats P2 as if it doesn't exist topologically — both arcs traverse through P2 without subdivision.

**The mechanism (inferred):** bikar's DCEL construction reads `intersect` declarations as the authoritative list of vertices. Geometric incidence alone is insufficient — the face-walker neither sweep-line-detects undeclared crossings nor splits arcs proactively at them. This is a deliberate design choice (likely for performance + author-intent fidelity) but it does mean strays accumulate silently as the pattern grows.

**How to apply:** (a) When authoring multi-circle compositions, **trust that undeclared circle-crossings stay dormant** — you do NOT need to add defensive `intersect` declarations for every geometric crossing; (b) when you DO want a crossing to split sub-faces (e.g., enrolling a stray to create a 3rd region), add the `intersect` declaration explicitly — bikar will not auto-detect; (c) for downstream consumers that compute geometric incidence (qiyas detector pixel-counting, sacred-patterns hierarchical diff): if they see a stray crossing in the rendered image but bikar's gt.json doesn't enumerate it as a vertex, that is **expected behavior**, not a defect — the consumer's geometric pass and bikar's declarative pass are correctly orthogonal; (d) when investigating "I see N faces in the render but bikar gt.json says M < N", check whether implicit strays are being counted by the consumer that bikar didn't enroll — that's the local-arc-enrollment boundary in action; (e) the rule is **stable across multi-lens N**: confirmed at N=2 lenses (Tick 78), confirmed at N=1 lens + polygon (Ticks 69-71), confirmed at N=N lenses on shared C0 (Ticks 72-74). Predicted to hold at higher N until incidence-degree-exceeds-4 hazard fires (Tick 75 boundary).
