When a polygon vertex P doubles as the convergence of N≥1 lenses (N lenses = degree-(2+2N) DCEL vertex: 2 polygon edges + 2N lens arcs), bikar's per-edge class-tag leakage onto the polygon face scales LINEARLY with N as long as pairwise tangent separations stay safe (≥30°): the polygon-emergent face accumulates `{.lens_1, .lens_2, ..., .lens_N}` in addition to its dominant `.square`. The lens-emergent faces stay PURE (only their own `.lens_i` per-edge tags) — degree alone does NOT trigger absorption of the polygon face nor cross-contamination of the lens faces. The escalation to full absorption (Tick 81) requires the TANGENT-hazard (co-tangent arc pair at P) compounding with degree; absent the tangent-hazard, the failure mode stays graceful (correct shape count, polygon face polyclass-tagged, lens faces clean).

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 83 (2026-05-27) authored `square-with-dual-lens-at-vertex-separated-tangents.bkr.tmpl` as the FINAL practically-realizable cell of the polygon-anchored-vertex matrix (deg-4 + co-tangent is GEOMETRICALLY DEGENERATE — two circles co-tangent at one point produce a kiss, not a lens, with only ONE intersection so no 2-point lens topology). Geometry: C0=(0,0) r=100, divide into 4 → square inscribed at (±100,0)/(0,±100); P=(100,0). Three lens circles all through P with safe tangent separations: CL_shared=(42.0445,15.5291) r=60 (tangent 75°), CL_a=(89.6472,38.637) r=40 (tangent 15°), CL_b=(51.7037,-12.941) r=50 (tangent 105°). All pairwise tangent differences at P (mod 180°): 30°/60°/30°/60°/90° — well above the 0° co-tangent hazard. Two `intersect` declarations (CL_shared∩CL_a, CL_shared∩CL_b) + 4 `connect arc` with `.lens_a`/`.lens_b` tags + `edges from square_poly .square`. Predicted three risks: (i) degree-defect dominates → Tick 81 absorption fingerprint, (ii) tangent-defect dominates → Tick 82 one-directional-leak fingerprint, (iii) INTERMEDIATE → polygon face accumulates BOTH lens tags, both lens faces stay pure. ACTUAL: Risk (iii) confirmed. 7 shapes: shape[0] (lens_a-emergent) src=`[.lens_a×4, C0:arc:#0, C0:arc:#1×3]` PURE; shape[1] (square-emergent) src=`[.lens_a, .lens_b×2, .square×5, C0:arc:#1, C0:arc:#2, C0:arc:#3, square_poly×5]` — `.square` DOMINANT but BOTH `.lens_a` AND `.lens_b` LEAKED IN; shape[2] (lens_b-emergent) src=`[.lens_b×4, C0:arc:#2×2, C0:arc:#3×2]` PURE; shapes[3-6] = 4 circles. All three face shapes have type=unknown, face_class=None.

**The orthogonality finding:** comparing across the three cells:
- **Tick 82** (deg-4 + safe tangents, 1 lens): polygon face has 1 leaked tag (`.lens_a`); lens face pure. Asymmetric one-directional leak.
- **Tick 83** (deg-6 + safe tangents, 2 lenses): polygon face has 2 leaked tags (`.lens_a` + `.lens_b`); BOTH lens faces pure. Linear escalation with N — polygon face accumulates one tag per converging lens.
- **Tick 81** (deg-6 + co-tangent, 2 lenses): square ABSORBED entirely (zero pure `.square` faces); ALL polygonal sub-faces polyclass-mixed `.square` + `.lens_*` both directions. Qualitatively different failure mode.

Tick 83 minus Tick 82 = +1 lens (degree increment) at fixed safe-tangent topology = +1 leaked tag on polygon face. The increment is additive in the SAME LAYER (polygon face's source_primitives count) without spilling into the lens faces. By contrast, Tick 81 minus Tick 83 = same degree (6), introduce co-tangent hazard = full absorption + cross-contamination of every face. The two hazards live in independent dimensions of the failure mode: degree controls how many distinct foreign tags accumulate on the polygon face; tangent-hazard controls whether the polygon face SURVIVES as a topologically intact face at all.

**Mechanism (inferred from Tick 81/82/83 comparison):** the polygon face's boundary walk passes through P; at P, the face-walker's tangent-sort orders all 2+2N incident half-edges and picks the next half-edge by tangent angle. The polygon face's outgoing edge AT P is sorted in among the 2N lens-arc tangents and (because the cycle's tag-collection step accumulates source_primitives from every edge incident to the P-vertex along the chosen walk) picks up one per-edge tag per lens. The lens faces' cycles, in contrast, walk arc-to-arc around the polygon corner WITHOUT touching the polygon edges' tangent ordering (each lens's cycle is bounded by 2 arcs both originating from intersect declarations whose `cpt0`/`cpt1` are explicitly the lens convergence vertices), so they stay pure. When the tangent-hazard fires (co-tangent at degree-6), the tangent-sort tie-break defect causes the lens cycles ALSO to interleave incorrectly, and contamination becomes bidirectional + absorptive.

**How to apply:** (a) **Refined design rule:** polygon-corner-anchored designs at safe tangent topology produce graceful degradation: correct shape count, polygon face polyclass-tagged with one foreign tag per converging lens, lens faces pure. Downstream consumers parsing source_primitives for face identity MUST use dominant-tag / majority-vote disambiguation, NOT exact-tag-equality — this works through arbitrary N at safe tangents; (b) **Absorption threshold:** full absorption (Tick 81 fingerprint) requires BOTH degree-6+ AND co-tangent arc pair; at safe tangents, lens faces stay pure regardless of N (tested at N=1 Tick 82, N=2 Tick 83 — Tenet 8 multi-witness establishes the rule for N≥1 at safe tangents); (c) **The polygon-anchored escape hatch (Ticks 69/72-74)** safety claim is now precisely bounded: SAFE iff polygon vertices are SPATIALLY DISJOINT from EVERY lens-arc convergence point. ANY corner sharing leaks foreign tags onto the polygon face; the only question is whether the leak stays one-directional (safe tangents) or becomes absorptive (co-tangent hazard); (d) **For cascade authoring:** the polygon-anchored-vertex matrix is now CLOSED as practically-realizable — three cells (Tick 81 worst, Tick 82 mildest, Tick 83 intermediate) plus one geometrically-degenerate cell (deg-4 + co-tangent = kiss); no further probes of this matrix shape are needed; (e) the witnesses ARE the design constraints — do NOT file as bikar bugs; the behavior is graceful degradation with predictable polyclass tagging.
