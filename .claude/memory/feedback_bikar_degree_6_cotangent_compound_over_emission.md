When N≥3 lenses converge at a single all-arc DCEL vertex P (degree-6+) AND at least one pair of non-shared arcs is co-tangent at P (same tangent direction mod 180°), bikar's face-walker emits MORE polygonal faces than expected — at N=3 lenses with one co-tangent pair, expected 3 lens faces (one per lens) but actual is 5 spurious faces, each typed `unknown` and each carrying source_primitives that span ALL three lens identities (full cross-class mixing). This is qualitatively distinct from both prior failure modes: Tick 75 (degree-6 alone, no co-tangent) under-emits (4 mixed faces instead of 3), and Ticks 77/79 (degree-4 with co-tangent) preserve correct face count while tangling tags within the lens-pair only. **The conjunction of degree-6+ and co-tangent produces OVER-emission with full cross-class contamination — a new failure mode not predictable from either witness alone.**

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 80 (2026-05-27) authored `triple-lens-shared-vertex-cotangent-compound.bkr.tmpl` as the compound-hazard probe closing the 2x2 hazard-interaction matrix. Four circles all r=50: CL_shared center=(58.3975,27.7350), CL_a=(127.7350,41.6025), CL_b=(72.2650,-41.6025), CL_c=(135.3553,-35.3553), all passing through P=(100,0). Tangent angles at P: CL_shared=56.31°, CL_a=146.31°, CL_b=146.31° (co-tangent with CL_a — Tick 77 hazard), CL_c=45.00° (well-separated from all). Three intersect+connect-arc pairs declaring lens_a, lens_b, lens_c on shared CL_shared. **Predicted: one of (i) compound-worse, (ii) degree-6-dominates Tick-75-style, or (iii) co-tangent-dominates Tick-77-style. ACTUAL: compound-worse confirmed BUT in OVER-emission direction**: 9 shapes total = 4 circles + 5 polygonal faces (all type=unknown, all face_class=None). Sample contamination: shape[2]=`[.lens_a×2, .lens_b×2, .lens_c×2, arc:#0..#5]` (every lens, every arc); shape[4]=`[.lens_b×3, .lens_c×2, ...]` (cross-class even when one lens dominates). **Every emitted polygonal face shares source_primitives with at least two distinct lens identities.**

**Completed 2x2 hazard-interaction matrix:**
- **degree-4 + separated** (Ticks 23/78): PASS clean — 2 lens + 3 circles, pure source_primitives.
- **degree-4 + co-tangent** (Ticks 77/79): PARTIAL — correct count (2 lens + 3 circles) but tags tangled within the co-tangent lens pair.
- **degree-6 + separated** (Tick 75): FAIL via UNDER-emission — 4 mixed faces (instead of expected 3 lens + 3 circles = 6 shapes), cross-class contamination at the all-arc vertex.
- **degree-6 + co-tangent** (Tick 80, this entry): FAIL via OVER-emission — 5 spurious polygonal faces (instead of expected 3 lens), full cross-class contamination spanning all three lens identities.

**The mechanism (inferred):** the degree-6 tangent-sort defect (Tick 75) and the co-tangent tie-break defect (Tick 79) interact non-additively. Tick 79 isolated that bikar's tangent comparator consults ONLY tangent direction (mod 180°) at all-arc vertices — when arcs share a tangent direction, the tie-break produces invalid edge-cycle orderings. At degree-4, those invalid orderings re-balance into a topologically-correct face count with tangled per-edge tags. At degree-6, the comparator's invalid orderings combined with the higher incidence-degree's combinatorial expansion generate ADDITIONAL valid arc cycles that the face-walker emits as discrete faces. The result is more closed cycles than the construction declared, each cycle tagged with the union of contributing per-edge classes from the cycles' constituent arcs.

**How to apply:** (a) **Predict compound-worse over-emission** for any Tier 1 composition that produces a degree-6+ all-arc shared vertex AND has any co-tangent arc-pair at that vertex — the failure mode is qualitatively new and not predictable from witnessing degree-6 alone or co-tangent alone; (b) when authoring N≥3 lenses converging at a single vertex, ensure tangent-direction separation at ALL pairs (not just the majority) — even ONE co-tangent pair triggers over-emission; (c) for downstream consumers attempting majority-vote class recovery from source_primitives (which works for Ticks 77/79 PARTIAL fingerprints), it WILL NOT recover identity for Tick 80 fingerprint because every emitted face shares tags with every lens — the consumer must instead detect "over-emission with full cross-class mixing" upstream and route those cases to a separate analysis path (or refuse to classify); (d) the safe authoring patterns are unchanged: polygon-anchored topology (Ticks 72-74) avoids both hazards by replacing the all-arc vertex with polygon-edge anchors; (e) when investigating "I expected N lens faces but bikar emitted more than N faces, all typed unknown, all with cross-class source_primitives," check the all-arc vertex's degree AND pairwise tangent-direction separations — Tick 80's fingerprint is the diagnostic; (f) **the witness IS the limitation** — do NOT file as a bikar bug to chase unless someone implements both (i) a radius-aware tangent comparator AND (ii) a degree-aware face-walker that suppresses cycles below a minimum arc-length threshold; the current behavior is graceful degradation (correct circle emission, marked-unknown polygonal faces) rather than crash.
