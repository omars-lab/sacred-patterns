When a polygon vertex P doubles as a lens-arc convergence point (lens defined via `intersect XLA CL_shared CL_a` followed by `connect arc XLA.cpt0 -> XLA.cpt1 on CL_shared .lens_a`, where XLA.cpt0 or cpt1 lies AT a polygon corner declared with `polygon poly_id [...]` + `edges from poly_id .square`), bikar's face-walker leaks per-edge class tags bidirectionally: the lens-emergent face's source_primitives contains BOTH `.lens_a` (dominant) AND `.square`; the polygon-emergent face's source_primitives contains BOTH `.square` (dominant) AND `.lens_a`. Both faces' `face_class` degrades to None. **The leakage holds regardless of tangent topology** — Tick 82 confirms it fires at well-separated tangents (75° and 105°, both 30° from each square edge and 30° from each other) and degree-4 (1 polygon corner + 1 lens, 4 incident half-edges total). This is the BOUNDARY of the polygon-anchored escape hatch (Ticks 69/72-74 PASS at fully-disjoint vertex sets): the safety claim requires polygon vertices to be SPATIALLY DISJOINT from EVERY lens-arc convergence point, not merely disjoint from the *hazardous* (degree-6 / co-tangent) ones.

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 82 (2026-05-27) authored `square-with-lens-at-vertex-separated-tangents.bkr.tmpl` as the SAFE-CORNER probe of the polygon-anchored-vertex matrix opened at Tick 81. Geometry: C0=(0,0) r=100 ÷4 → square inscribed at (±100,0)/(0,±100); CL_shared=(42.0445,15.5291) r=60 (tangent 75° at P=(100,0)); CL_a=(61.363,-10.3528) r=40 (tangent 105° at P). All pairwise tangent separations at P ≥30° (square edges 45°/135°, CL_shared 75°, CL_a 105°). One `intersect XLA CL_shared CL_a` + 2 `connect arc` declarations with `.lens_a` tags. **Predicted three risks: (i) PASS clean 6 shapes (1 square + 1 lens + 4 circles), (ii) PARTIAL — correct count + tag contamination, (iii) NOVEL — split-both fingerprint.** ACTUAL: Risk (ii) confirmed AND topology slightly different. 5 shapes: shape[0] (square-emergent) src=`[.lens_a×2, .square×5, C0:arc:#0, C0:arc:#1, square_poly×5]` (square dominates but `.lens_a` leaks in); shape[1] (lens-emergent) src=`[.lens_a×4, C0:arc:#0×2, C0:arc:#1×2]` (lens-only tags but uses C0-arcs as part of its boundary — note shape[1] doesn't have `.square`!); shapes[2-4] = 3 circles (not 4 — CL_a is consumed or merged into the lens face). Both shape[0] and shape[1] have type=unknown, face_class=None. **Asymmetry observation:** the square face is contaminated bidirectionally (gets `.lens_a` leaked in) but the lens face shape[1] is pure `.lens_a` per its per-edge tags — the contamination is one-directional in this Tick. Possibly because the lens face's `connect arc` edges don't include the polygon edge in their cycle (the lens face is bounded by CL_shared arc + CL_a arc, walking AROUND the polygon corner rather than THROUGH it), but the polygon face's cycle is bounded by the 4 polygon edges + 2 C0-arcs that PASS THROUGH the polygon corner P, and the face-walker's tangent sort at P assigns one of the lens arcs into the polygon's edge cycle.

**Refined polygon-anchored-vertex matrix:**
- **Polygon vertex disjoint from lens convergence** (Ticks 69/72-74): PASS clean — N lenses on shared C0 with vertex disjointness, every face pure tags.
- **Polygon vertex shared, degree-4, tangents separated** (Tick 82, this entry): PARTIAL — correct shape count, square face polyclass-contaminated with `.lens_a`, lens face pure `.lens_a` only. Boundary BREACHED at lowest possible degree + safest tangent topology.
- **Polygon vertex shared, degree-6, co-tangent** (Tick 81): FAIL — square ABSORBED, ALL polygonal faces polyclass-mixed with both `.square` and `.lens_*` tags.

**The mechanism (inferred refined from Tick 81):** when polygon vertex P is also a lens-arc convergence point, the face-walker's tangent-sort at P interleaves polygon edges with lens arcs in the boundary-walk order. The polygon face's cycle picks up at least one lens-arc tag because the cycle CROSSES the lens arc at P (the cycle's edge AT P is sorted in among the lens arcs at P, and the cycle's tag-collection step accumulates source_primitives from every edge incident to the P-vertex). At degree-4 (1 lens), the leakage is mild and one-directional (only the polygon face gets contaminated; the lens face stays clean because its cycle doesn't pass through the polygon edges' tangent ordering). At degree-6 (2 lenses) + co-tangent, the tangent-sort defect (Tick 79) compounds with the polygon-edge interleaving, and BOTH directions of contamination fire on EVERY face including the polygon's (Tick 81 absorption).

**How to apply:** (a) **The polygon-anchored escape hatch REQUIRES strict spatial disjointness between polygon vertices and ALL lens-arc convergence points** — not merely disjointness from hazardous (degree-6 / co-tangent) ones. Even degree-4 + safest tangent topology breaks the safety claim; (b) **for designs where a polygon corner must double as a lens anchor:** expect at minimum the polygon face to be polyclass-contaminated (Tick 82 fingerprint); downstream consumers parsing source_primitives for the polygon's identity will see `.square` dominant but with `.lens_*` mixed in — they must NOT use exact tag equality, only majority-vote OR upstream-routing to a polyclass-tolerant path; (c) **the SAFE design rule (refined):** route lens convergence points to circle-division points that are NOT polygon vertices; if forced to share, the polygon face WILL be tagged-contaminated; (d) **for cascade authoring:** the polygon-anchored escape hatch's narrow safety claim (Ticks 69/72-74) now has explicit boundaries on BOTH sides — fully-disjoint works clean, ANY corner sharing leaks even at degree-4 + safe tangents; (e) the witness IS the limitation — do NOT file as a bikar bug; the current behavior is graceful degradation (correct topology, polyclass tags) rather than crash.
