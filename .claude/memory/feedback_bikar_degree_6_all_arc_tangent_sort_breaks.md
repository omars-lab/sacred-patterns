When THREE OR MORE lens primitives share a SINGLE all-arc DCEL vertex (every incident half-edge is a circular arc, no polygon chord edges present), bikar's face-walker produces TANGLED face emission. Predict cross-lens source_primitives, mismatched face counts, and incorrect cyclic next-edge ordering — the half-edge tangent-angle sort that works at degree-4 all-arc (Tick 23 PASS) breaks at degree-6 all-arc. **Do NOT author Tier 1 templates with ≥3 lenses converging on one shared topological point as if they were independent lens witnesses** — they are NOT, and the gt.json output will misclassify the topology silently (face_class=None for all 4 emitted faces).

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 75 (2026-05-27) authored `triple-lens-shared-vertex.bkr.tmpl`. Construction: CL_shared (r=50), CL_a (r=30), CL_b (r=45), CL_c (r=40), ALL passing through P=(100,0). Three `intersect XL_n CL_shared CL_n` + three pairs of `connect arc` with DISTINCT per-edge class tags (.lens_a / .lens_b / .lens_c). Predicted 7 shapes (3 lenses + 4 circles); ACTUAL: 8 shapes (4 face shapes + 4 circles). The 4 emitted faces have CROSS-LENS source_primitives: shape[0] = {.lens_a×2, .lens_c×2, arc:#0}; shape[1] = {.lens_a×2, .lens_c, arc:#0, arc:#1}; shape[2] = {.lens_a, .lens_c×4, ...}; shape[3] = {.lens_b×3, .lens_c, arc:#2}. All four have `face_class=None` (no clean class disambiguation). The expected `(1 lens_a + 1 lens_b + 1 lens_c)` clean-tag set is destroyed. **This contradicts the cross-lens generalization of [[feedback_bikar_arc_enrollment_local_not_propagated]]** — the local-arc-enrollment rule was witnessed at:
- N=1 lens on shared C0 + polygon (Ticks 69/70/71): PASS
- N=2 lenses on shared C0 + polygon, spatially disjoint (Tick 72): PASS
- N=3 lenses on shared C0 + polygon, spatially disjoint (Tick 73): PASS
- 2 lenses sharing ONE all-arc vertex, degree-4 incidence (Tick 23): PASS
- **3 lenses sharing ONE all-arc vertex, degree-6 incidence (Tick 75): FAIL**

The discriminator is NOT "N lenses on shared circle" (which is fine when spatially disjoint and polygon-anchored) — it's "N≥3 lenses converging at ONE all-arc DCEL vertex." Tick 23 flagged the risk explicitly in its header ("a regression where the tangent-angle sort works for ≤2 arcs at a vertex but breaks for ≥3 arcs would be invisible to every prior arc-bearing entry"); Tick 75 promoted the risk to a witnessed failure.

**How to apply:** When authoring Tier 1 multi-lens compositions that share ONE topological point (not spatially disjoint lenses, which are independent per Ticks 72/73): (a) if the shared point has degree ≥6 of incident arc half-edges (3+ lens circles all passing through it), do NOT predict clean per-lens face emission — predict tangled cross-references and report the degeneracy as part of the witness (negative-witness Tier 1 entry, useful for detector calibration); (b) if you NEED clean per-lens faces at a multi-lens vertex, restrict to ≤2 lenses (degree-4 incidence is safe per Tick 23); (c) if you need 3+ lenses topologically connected, route them through a polygon (Ticks 69-73 path) or spatially disjoint on shared C0 (Ticks 72/73 path) rather than converging at one vertex; (d) when the test verdict is "8 shapes vs predicted 7" or similar +1 deviations, check source_primitives for cross-class mixing before assuming a face-walker BUG vs. a face-walker LIMITATION at high all-arc incidence. **This entry IS the witness** — file as known limitation of bikar's tangent-angle ordering, not a bug to chase (until/unless someone implements a more robust comparator for the all-arc vertex case).
