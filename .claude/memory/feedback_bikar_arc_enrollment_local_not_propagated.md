When authoring Tier 1 polygon+lens entries that BOTH reference the same construction circle (path-d of the Tick 21 enumeration: shared-circle, spatially disjoint, no shared vertex/edge), bikar's face-walker enrolls only the explicitly-declared `connect arc` boundary into the lens face. Neighboring C0 sub-arcs between the polygon's divide-point vertices and the lens's `intersect`-derived endpoints remain DORMANT — they do not propagate enrollment to form a "cap" face between the polygon's chord edge and the lens's C0-arc. Predict polygon-and-lens-shared-circle compositions as `(1 polygon + 1 lens + 2 circles)` shapes when the lens lies entirely on the polygon-exterior side of any polygon chord, with no vertex/edge coincidence.

**Why:** qiyas#132 Tick 69 (2026-05-27) authored `square-and-lens-shared-circle.bkr.tmpl`. Construction: C0 r=100 ÷4 (inscribed square via `polygon [C0.cpt0 C0.cpt1 C0.cpt2 C0.cpt3]`), CL=(90,90) r=30, `intersect XL C0 CL` produces XL.cpt0≈(78.09, 62.47) and XL.cpt1≈(62.47, 78.09), `connect arc XL.cpt0 -> XL.cpt1 on C0 .arc_lens` + `connect arc XL.cpt1 -> XL.cpt0 on CL .arc_lens`. Lens endpoints sit at C0 angles 38.66°/51.34°, strictly between square vertices cpt0 (0°) and cpt1 (90°); lens arc-midpoint at angle 45° on C0 = (70.71, 70.71) lies outside the square's chord cpt0→cpt1 (whose midpoint is (50,50) at distance 70.71 from origin — lens is on the polygon-exterior side). Predicted output: 4 shapes (square + lens + 2 circles). MAIN RISK: bikar might opportunistically enroll the C0 sub-arc 0°→38.66° (between cpt0 and XL.cpt0) and 51.34°→90° (between XL.cpt1 and cpt1), forming a "cap" face between the square's chord cpt0→cpt1 and the C0-arc cpt0→XL.cpt0→XL.cpt1→cpt1 (with the lens C0-arc filling the middle), yielding 3 face shapes (square split into chord-side + cap). ACTUAL OUTPUT: 4 shapes — square whole (gt_G0000: vertex_count=4, has_arcs=false, .square), lens (gt_G0001: vertex_count=2, has_arcs=true, .arc_lens), 2 circles. Square emitted as 4 line-edges only; the C0 sub-arcs adjacent to the lens are dormant. Refines [[feedback_bikar_face_walker_shared_edge_absorption_universal]] Tick 25's conditional-enrollment principle with a tighter boundary: arc enrollment is LOCAL to the explicitly-declared `connect arc` boundary, NOT propagated to neighboring sub-arcs on the same circle even when those sub-arcs share endpoints with named division points.

**How to apply:** When authoring Tier 1 polygon+lens shared-circle compositions: (a) verify lens endpoints (from `intersect`) don't coincide with polygon vertices (avoids Tick 67 arc-through-polygon-vertex hazard); (b) verify the lens arc-midpoint lies on the polygon-exterior side of every polygon chord adjacent to its endpoints (so the lens doesn't intrude into polygon interior — would trigger the Tick 50 asymmetric-lens-intrudes pattern instead); (c) heterogeneous sides (polygon=N, lens=2) to escape Tick 9 polyclass trap; (d) predict (1 polygon + 1 lens + 2 circles) shape count with confidence. This path-d composition is the LEAST-coupled cross-primitive Tier 1 — no shared topology beyond the underlying circle — and bikar handles it cleanly, completing the path-d witness in the Tick 21 path enumeration.
