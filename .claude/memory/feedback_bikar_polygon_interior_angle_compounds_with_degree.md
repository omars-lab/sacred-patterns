When a polygon vertex P doubles as the convergence of 2 lenses at safe-separation tangents (≥30° pairwise), the leakage/absorption fingerprint depends on the polygon's INTERIOR ANGLE at P. For a square (interior angle 90° at P), the polygon face survives intact with linear-accumulation of leaked lens tags (Tick 83: polygon face has BOTH lens tags, lens faces stay PURE — 7 shapes). For a triangle (interior angle 60° at P) with otherwise identical structural setup (same C0 r=100, same 3 lens circles with tangents 90°/60°/0° at P, same intersect+connect arc pattern, same edges-from polygon class tag), the polygon is ABSORBED — Tick 81 fingerprint fires at SAFE tangent topology, 8 shapes (4 polyclass-mixed face shapes including ZERO pure `.triangle`, + 4 circles), every face has cross-class source_primitives. The polygon interior angle is therefore a NEW load-bearing variable for the absorption threshold, distinct from the degree-hazard and tangent-hazard previously isolated; absorption fires when interior angle is NARROW even without the tangent-hazard. **The Tick 83 orthogonality finding (degree ⊥ tangent at polygon corners with linear-accumulation) is SQUARE-SPECIFIC and does NOT generalize to other polygon symmetries.**

But a narrow interior angle (60° at the triangle vertex) is sufficient to trigger absorption WITHOUT the tangent-hazard at the same degree.

**Mechanism (inferred):** the face-walker's tangent-sort at degree-(2+2N) vertex P interleaves all 2+2N incident half-edges by tangent angle. The polygon edges at P sit at angles 30° and 150° (triangle) or 45° and 135° (square). The 4 lens half-edges at P sit at angles 0°, 60°, 90° (and another reverse direction). For the SQUARE, the polygon-edge tangents (45°/135°) bracket the lens tangents in a way that lets the polygon face's boundary walk pick the correct "next" half-edge cleanly — the polygon face emerges with `.triangle`-equivalent dominant tags + 2 leaked lens tags. For the TRIANGLE, the polygon-edge tangents (30°/150°) sit ON TOP OF lens tangents 0°/60° (with separations exactly = 30°, the minimum allowed) — the tangent-sort tie-break at these minimum-separation pairs becomes unstable, causing the face-walker to mis-route the polygon face's boundary walk into lens cycles, absorbing the polygon. The 30° tangent-separation between polygon edges and lens arcs is sufficient to be "safe" in the original spec, but it's the SAME separation as the minimum-separation pair triggering the tie-break failure in Tick 79 (asymmetric+co-tangent). **The minimum-pairwise-tangent-separation matters across polygon AND lens — not just within-lens.** A 30° polygon-edge-vs-lens tangent separation at degree-6 is enough to trigger absorption.
